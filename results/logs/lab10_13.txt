------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| tariff        | static workflow | RSIFlow meta-agent | adaptive - static (wins) |
|---------------|-----------------|--------------------|--------------------------|
| tou           | 0.896 +/- 0.049 | 0.895 +/- 0.034    | -0.000 (3/8)             |
| critical_peak | 0.943 +/- 0.012 | 0.932 +/- 0.006    | -0.011 (1/8)             |
| wholesale     | 0.778 +/- 0.028 | 0.773 +/- 0.036    | -0.005 (5/8)             |
| solar_glut    | 0.974 +/- 0.004 | 0.972 +/- 0.004    | -0.002 (3/8)             |

  mean adaptive - static over tariffs: -0.004.
  Why so little? In the example run the bottleneck was 'wasted_solar' in 56/58 steps: the
  symptom signal barely changes, so there is little to adapt TO, and UCB still pays to explore
  every branch for that one bottleneck. RSIFlow's premise -- bottlenecks that EVOLVE -- is weak on
  this task; the static recipe already spreads effort across the useful actions.
