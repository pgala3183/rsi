------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| 12 training days chosen by | held-out capture after 80 evaluations |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| learnable (max p(1-p))     | 0.794 +/- 0.101                       |
| hardest (lowest p)         | 0.877 +/- 0.035                       |
| easiest (highest p)        | 0.739 +/- 0.077                       |
| random 12 days             | 0.913 +/- 0.032                       |

  Learnability picks days whose outcomes DIFFER across the population -- a narrow slice of day types.
  Optimising on that slice alone transfers worse than a random sample: learnability is a good signal
  for what to ADD to a curriculum, not a replacement for a representative one.

5. Summary: what each metric solves, when it is useful, its limits (with our numbers)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
| metric                    | problem it solves                                              | useful when                                      | limitation                                                                                                     |
|---------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| RSI-Index                 | compares improvement across benchmarks of different difficulty | reporting one number over a suite                | near-solved benchmarks dominate; hides absolute gains; 'ideal' must exist                                      |
| steering-split solve rate | cheap proxy used for selection during search                   | when it ranks like held-out (Spearman 0.94 here) | the selected node is optimistic by +0.037; solve counts are coarser than means                                 |
| Recursive Learnability    | chooses practice tasks that separate good from bad solvers     | self-play curricula without labels               | here a learnable-only curriculum scored 0.794 vs random 0.913: it is unrepresentative of the test distribution |
| swarm diversity           | detects premature convergence of a swarm                       | deciding when to inject new hypotheses           | syntactic distance can hide behavioural sameness                                                               |
